Investment philosophy

Max tax advantage first
Triple-stack IRA + 401(k) + HSA before anything else. The tax savings compound just as hard as the returns.
Factor investing
QVAL, QMOM, IVAL, IMOM — systematic quant factor ETFs for value and momentum exposure. Evidence-based, not gut-feel.
High-conviction stocks
Every position has a documented thesis. GOOG held since 2022. ASML for EUV monopoly. SHOP for e-commerce infrastructure.
Long time horizon
Started at 19. The 2065 target date on the 401(k) says it all. Volatility is noise — compounding is the signal.
Track everything
Cost basis on every lot. Sector weights. Net worth snapshot. You can’t manage what you don’t measure.
Geographic diversification
IVAL + IMOM for international factor exposure. FTIHX in HSA for global ex-US. US-heavy but not US-only.

Inside the spreadsheet

Built in Excel with live formula linking across all tabs — change a value in Holdings and it cascades to Dashboard, Allocation, and Net Worth instantly. Every position has a documented investment thesis. Every account is tracked with cost basis and unrealized gain/loss. The goal: one source of truth for my entire financial picture.

Data flow

Holdings31 positions, cost basis + value
→
Dashboardlive totals, =SUM(Holdings)
→
Allocationsector weights, auto-recalc
→
Net WorthIRA + 401k + HSA + savings − debt

Every value in the model is formula-driven — no manual entry after the initial setup. Update a position in Holdings and the Dashboard total, sector allocation percentage, and net worth snapshot all recalculate instantly. This mirrors the single-source-of-truth principle used in relational database design.
