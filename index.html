Event operations, moving into accounting

I’m an Event Services Specialist at Maritz, working on site at Google’s corporate event centers in Mountain View. For four years my job has been taking large events from a plan to the day itself: the spaces, the vendors, catering and AV, and the problems that come up once people are in the room. On event days, it’s a lot like air traffic control.

Alongside the job, I’m studying accounting at San Jose City College, with a transfer to San José State planned for spring 2029. I explored a lot of fields before landing on accounting. I like that it’s systematic. The part of the field I keep reading about is forensic accounting and fraud examination.

Where I’m headed

What interests me most in accounting is the investigative side: tracing where money actually went, finding the entries that don’t line up, and understanding how fraud happens inside an organization and how it gets caught, or doesn’t. Forensic accounting and fraud examination are the fields I’m working toward.

Next to that, I’m interested in accounting information systems, internal controls and IT audit, because that is where most of the evidence lives and where the controls either hold or fail.

For now I’m partway through the degree, reading a lot, and building things to get closer to the material. My working experience so far is four years in event operations, and you’ll find it below.

Clohive

Product exploration, UX design and user research planning

When I started my first professional job, I didn’t know how to dress for it. I learned by watching YouTube videos, looking up which colors go together, browsing outfit ideas and asking my family what worked. Clohive was an idea for an app that would bring that search into one place for young men working out their style.

At the time I thought I wanted to work in product management, so I wrote a research plan and designed the main screens. A paid survey came back with responses I couldn’t trust, so I didn’t use them, and I lost interest before the idea was ever tested with users.
